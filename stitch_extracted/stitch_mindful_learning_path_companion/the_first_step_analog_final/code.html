The Alchemy of Form

The transition from mere observation to active interpretation is where the true craft begins. We've traced the lines of the visible world, but now we must distill its essence.

Consider the objects on a shelf. They are not simply containers of matter, but vessels of light and shadow, each holding a specific gravity within the composition.

Notice how the edges blur where the light wraps around the form, and sharpen where shadow defines the boundary. This is not an error of the eye, but the poetry of perception.

"The snail slime serum always catches the highlight. Remember to keep the strokes loose here."

- M.
