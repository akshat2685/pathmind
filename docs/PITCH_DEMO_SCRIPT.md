This script outlines a concise, deterministic 5-minute live demonstration of PATHMIND. It proves the core differentiation without fake data, simulated personas, or pre-canned video recordings.

## Demonstration Narrative: 10 Core Milestones

### 1. Who is the Person? (Authenticated Persona Identity)
* **Action**: Open the PATHMIND Home / Initiation view (`/`). Show the TopBar profile badge.
* **Talking Point**: *"PATHMIND begins with the authenticated individual. There are no pre-seeded dummy profiles or fake personas. Every insight is scoped to this authentic user identity via strict server-side multi-tenant boundaries."*

### 2. What are They Trying to Achieve? (Curiosity & Direction)
* **Action**: In the Initiation prompt, enter: `"Machine Learning & Applied AI Systems Specialist"`, click **Embark**.
* **Talking Point**: *"The user declares their genuine curiosity. Unlike a static curriculum, PATHMIND treats career direction as an evolving hypothesis."*

### 3. What Does PATHMIND Know About Them? (Personal Context Graph)
* **Action**: Navigate to `/onboarding` or `/readiness`. Show the Context Command Center.
* **Talking Point**: *"The Personal Context Graph links user background, academic foundation, declared constraints (e.g. 12 hours/week), and verified capabilities into a coherent cognitive model."*

### 4. What Does the System Recommend? (Counseling & Trajectory Discovery)
* **Action**: Navigate to `/explorer`. Review Candidate Trajectory Paths discovered from empirical case studies.
* **Talking Point**: *"PATHMIND decomposes the target outcome into candidate pathways, drawing on historical transition data. Notice that every path displays an honest fit rationale grounded in the user's assessed background."*

### 5. Progressive Roadmap Generation & Lock Enforcement
* **Action**: Select the Recommended AI Engineering Path and view the Learning Journey (`/journey`).
* **Talking Point**: *"Stage 1 (Python Foundations & Systems OOP) is ACTIVE. Click on Stage 2 or Stage 3. Notice they are strictly LOCKED. PATHMIND enforces progressive disclosure on the backend—you cannot check boxes or skip ahead without submitting verifiable proof."*

### 6. Submitting Real Evidence (The Evidence & Mastery Engine)
* **Action**: Open the active mission in Stage 1 (`/execution` or `/journey`). Submit real Python code implementing a batching generator with passing unit test assertions.
* **Talking Point**: *"PATHMIND does not ask 'Did you finish?'. It inspects the submitted artifact—analyzing syntax, type annotations, and passing test suites. Upon submission, the engine evaluates the evidence in real time."*

### 7. Evidence-Driven Mastery & Downstream Milestone Unlock
* **Action**: View `/evidence` (Mastery Dashboard).
* **Talking Point**: *"Stage 1 is now marked DEMONSTRATED_MASTERY. Stage 2 (Mathematics & Linear Algebra) automatically unlocks. Progress in PATHMIND is empirical proof of competency, not a checkbox streak."*

### 8. The Adaptive Replanning Engine (Reality-Driven Adaptation)
* **Action**: Simulate a real-life constraint change: reduced time from 15 hrs/week down to 8 hrs/week due to university exams.
* **Talking Point**: *"Life happens. Instead of a broken schedule, the Adaptive Replanning Engine performs an immediate Impact Analysis. It recalculates the completion timeline, reschedules daily missions, and preserves 100% of all previously mastered milestones. Crucially: the system recommends; the person approves."*

### 9. Connecting to Real Opportunities (Fit vs. Readiness Separation)
* **Action**: Navigate to Opportunity Navigator (`/opportunities`). Inspect the top matched role.
* **Talking Point**: *"Look at this opportunity. PATHMIND separates 'Fit' (do you belong here?) from 'Readiness' (can you perform today?). It highlights exactly which requirements your verified evidence satisfies, which skills remain gaps, and flags unknown fields honestly rather than guessing with fake percentages."*

### 10. The Persistent Second Brain (Cross-Session Memory)
* **Action**: Navigate to Memory Vault (`/memory`). Ask: *"How did I implement batching?"*
* **Talking Point**: *"Notice the Second Brain retrieves the exact milestone breakthrough from earlier. If we ask about something we haven't done, it honestly states 'NO_RELEVANT_MEMORY'. PATHMIND is not an ephemeral chat prompt—it is a persistent, evidence-grounded copilot that grows longitudinally with you."*

1. **The Lock Enforcement Moment**: Clicking a locked downstream stage returns an authentic backend 403 error until passing evidence is submitted and verified.
2. **The Dynamic Replanning Moment**: Changing a time constraint triggers an explainable impact analysis with unchanged assets preserved.
3. **The Honest Opportunity Moment**: A job match separates fit from readiness, presenting concrete next steps without fabricated vanity scores.
